Who we are and what we want to do

Hello, and thank you for taking a moment to listen.

We are four master's students in Applied Computing at the University of Windsor: Abhinav Pandey, Shrijana Karki, Aakriti Mainali and Khemraj Adhikari.

We want to build something useful for blind and low vision students. Something that makes it easier to learn from the same material everyone else uses, whether that is lecture slides, research papers, articles online, complex math, graphs and diagrams, or content on braille devices like the Monarch.

But we do not want to guess what that should be. Too many tools are built without asking the people who will use them. So before we build anything, we want to hear from you. What gets in the way today? What already works? What would actually make a difference?

We are asking for about fifteen to thirty minutes. We would love to meet you, in person or online, or you can share your thoughts on this page instead. If you support students, or are a student yourself, your experience will shape what we build.

If the current tools and services already work well for you, please tell us that too. It helps us put our effort where it matters.

Taking part is completely voluntary. You can skip anything and stop at any time. We will only use what you share for this project, and we will never use your name unless you say we can.

Thank you. We hope to build something with you, not just for you.

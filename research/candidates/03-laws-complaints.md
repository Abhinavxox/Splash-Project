### AODA IASR s.15 - educational institutions (course materials)
https://accessiblecampus.ca/administrators/iasr/information-communications/section-15/
type: regulation (guidance page on the regulation, not the e-laws text itself)
notes:
- s.15(1) first bullet: when a person gives notice of need, institution must provide course/training resources in a format that accounts for disability. try to buy/get an accessible or conversion-ready electronic version first, if not then arrange a comparable one
- s.15(1) 2nd bullet: student records + program info in accessible format on request
- s.15(2) says which orgs count (Education Act, Private Career Colleges Act 2005, etc)
- page also says faculty picking course materials should know they must be accessible
supports claim: 5, 1
how solid: medium. it's an official-ish accessibility org (accessiblecampus, Ontario sector site), but I could NOT open e-laws to read the reg text itself (ontario.ca/laws/regulation/110191 only gave the title). quote the e-laws text before using it in the paper.

### AODA IASR s.17 - producers of textbooks / print supplementary material
https://accessiblecampus.ca/administrators/iasr/information-communications/section-17/
type: regulation (guidance page)
notes:
- s.17(1): textbook producers must supply accessible or conversion-ready version when institution asks. deadline listed as Jan 1 2015
- s.17(2): same for print supplementary learning resources, deadline Jan 1 2020
- only applies on request
supports claim: 5, 4
how solid: medium. same caveat as s.15, guidance site not e-laws. note it covers print, not slides/PDFs directly, so it's a stretch for lecture slides. useful for the "publisher has to supply it" angle.

### DOJ ADA Title II web + mobile app rule (2024) - ada.gov summary page
https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/
type: regulation (summary of federal rule, from DOJ)
notes:
- final rule published in Federal Register Apr 24 2024
- standard = WCAG 2.1 AA
- compliance dates as of the 2026 IFR: pop 50,000+ = Apr 26 2027; pop under 50,000 + special districts = Apr 26 2028
- DOJ page was the only read here, not the FR text itself (the govinfo 2024 PDF came back as raw compressed bytes)
supports claim: 5
how solid: strong. official DOJ page. but note: this is for state/local govt, NOT universities directly (public univs count as state/local gov so they do fall under Title II, but check that wording before the paper says it).

### DOJ interim final rule extending Title II compliance dates (Apr 20 2026)
https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-04-20/html/2026-07663.htm
type: regulation (Federal Register, interim final rule)
notes:
- extends the dates above: 50k+ moved Apr 24 2026 -> Apr 26 2027; under 50k moved Apr 26 2027 -> Apr 26 2028
- WCAG 2.1 AA unchanged
- comments were due June 22 2026
- IMPORTANT for the paper: the deadline moved, so say that explicitly. dont cite the old Apr 2026 date as current
supports claim: 5
how solid: strong. official Federal Register, opened and read.

### Penn State - OCR Early Complaint Resolution w/ NFB (docket #03-11-2020)
https://accessibility.psu.edu/ati/nfbpsusettlement
type: complaint or settlement (university-hosted summary of an OCR agreement)
notes:
- OCR docket 03-11-2020, NFB complaint about blind access
- requires EIT audit for blind users, WCAG 2.0 AA for websites and library tools, accessible procurement
- course management system to be replaced with accessible one (Section 508), phased
- does NOT talk about course materials/slides directly. the closest is the LMS part
- dates have passed (2011-2014 era), page says kept for archive
supports claim: 1, 6, 5
how solid: medium. real case, opened the page. but its a university's own summary, not the OCR letter itself, and it's old. good as precedent for "institution must fix its tools," not for "slides must be accessible."

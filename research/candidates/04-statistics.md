### canada at a glance disability (statcan)
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/pub/12-581-x/2023001/sec5-eng.htm
accessed 2026-10-29
type: government stats
notes:
- 2022 CSD: 27.0% of canadians 15+ have a disability (~8.0M), up from 22.3% in 2017
- seeing disability = 27.4% of people w/ disabilities (2022), up from 24.3% (2017). no count given, its % of PWD not all canadians
- employed 25-64: 61.8% w/ disability vs 77.8% without (2022)
- page is archived, no education numbers on it
supports claim: 7 scale
how solid: strong, indirect (general disability, not vision-specific for education)

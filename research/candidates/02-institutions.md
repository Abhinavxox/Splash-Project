### stanford OAE accessible format remediation page
https://oae.stanford.edu/faculty-staff/accessible-format-remediation-alternate-format/accessible-format-remediation
type: university page
notes:
- lecture slides: "OAE needs a minimum of 2 full business days to convert these materials into an accessible format."
- readings: "minimum of 5 full business days"; problem sets/handouts: "minimum of 3 full business days"
- textbooks/course files: "OAE can adapt textbooks and course files into accessible formats", uses OCR on original files to extract text
- also "Books that are used extensively in a quarter need to be identified before the quarter starts."
supports claim: 3 long waits, 4 manual fixing costly, 2 slides lose formulas tables diagrams (maybe, only indirectly), 5 institutions required (sort of, they do it as a service)
how solid: strong - it's an official university page w/ actual numbers. but the numbers are minimums and it's stanford (big remediation unit) so it's not every school.
goes against us: 2 business days for slides is FAST. if someone reads this they'll say "the school does it in 2 days, so where's the long wait?" need to frame it as: minimum, not average, and it only works bc stanford has a whole team (see the job postings idea).

### McGraw Hill - alternate file format request
https://www.mheducation.ca/higher-education/educators/accessibility/alternate-file-format-request
type: university page (no, it's a PUBLISHER page - be careful how we label it in the paper)
notes:
- "the turnaround time to receive the files is usually two to ten business days" (after the school submits the form)
- AccessText Network route: "Turnaround time is typically 2-3 days."
- so the publisher is the one doing the conversion, school just forwards it
supports claim: 3 long waits, 5 institutions required (publishers + schools have to do this for the request)
how solid: medium. real page, real numbers, but it's a vendor not an institution, and it's about textbooks not lecture slides. don't call it a university source.
goes against us: 2-3 days via AccessText is quick. "usually 2-10" is still a range where 10 business days = 2 weeks.

### UMaryland - request for alternate format textbooks
https://www.umaryland.edu/disabilityservices/for-students/request-for-alternate-format-textbooks/
type: university page
notes:
- "Please allow 10 business days to receive the alternative format textbook."

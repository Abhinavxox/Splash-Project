# Research questions

The problem research has to find which of these is the real obstacle. Each question has what we already have and who can answer it. A question is answered only with evidence, not opinion.

## Main questions

1. For whom. Which students struggle with lecture material, and in which courses?
2. Objectives. What are they trying to do with the material?
3. Difficulties. Where exactly do they get stuck?
4. Why. What causes it?
5. Consequences. What does it cost them?
6. How. Only after 1 to 5.

## Possible causes to test

| # | Question | What we have so far | Who can answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Is the content itself unreadable, mainly math, graphs, diagrams and image only slides? | Strong. Our scan, studies, students, scientists and posts all report it | Students, SAS |
| B | Does material arrive too late? | Mixed. Students and studies report delays, published turnaround is 2 to 10 business days | Students, SAS |
| C | Does fixing material cost staff or professors too much time? | Some. Job postings and tool reviews show manual work, no measured hours yet | SAS, instructors |
| D | Do instructors know how to make material accessible, or that they need to? | Repeated in studies and student accounts | Instructors, SAS |
| E | Do instructors share PDFs when the original file would work better? | Posts suggest it | Instructors, students |
| F | Do students' devices and tools, such as screen readers, braille displays and the Monarch, fail to support the material? | Partly. Tools work when content is structured, fail on images and untagged PDFs | Students, SAS |
| G | Does the SAS request process itself cause the delay? | Unknown | SAS, students |
| H | Are live lectures the bigger problem, board work and pointing, rather than files? | Some posts and student accounts | Students |
| I | Is the main barrier attitudes and low expectations rather than material? | Some scientists say so | Students, scientists |
| J | How much extra time does a student spend because of this? | A few examples, 30 minutes for a 5 minute video, 12 hours a week for statistics | Students |
| K | Is it actually not a problem here, because SAS already handles it well? | Unknown | SAS, students |

## How we close each question

- Ask SAS about A, B, C, F, G, K.
- Ask students about A, B, F, H, I, J, K.
- Ask instructors about C, D, E.
- Record each answer in `interviews/` with the question letter, and update this table with the result.

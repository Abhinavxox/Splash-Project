**Q1. In agile teams people often say engineers decide how the work gets done and the manager just clears blockers. Do you think that's really how it works in most teams?**

I think its team decisions most of the time so work is done with collaboration and joint discussions.

**Q2. In a lot of companies the team lead or manager talks to customers and passes priorities down to developers. Do you think that's still common, or do developers talk to customers directly more now?**

I still think leads and managers talk to customers and passes priorities to developers during sprint planning.

**Q3. Do you think a good engineer naturally makes a good manager? What changes when someone moves from engineering into management?**

Good engineer naturally maynot make good manager, because engineer maynot have leadership experience or skills. If engineers have or develop leadership skills then they can be naturally good managers.

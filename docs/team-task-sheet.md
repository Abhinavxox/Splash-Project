- Everyone has a task with a deadline every day.
- Every finished task gets a row in `docs/daily-log.md`, committed from your own GitHub account the same day.
- Reports are written in past tense. What we did, what we found, what we decided.

**New sources**

If you find anything new, a YouTube video, podcast, paper, article or published interview, add it. Put a numbered entry at the end of `research/reading-list.md` with the title and link, write two or three lines of notes on what it says in your summary file, and log it in `docs/daily-log.md`. Only add what you actually watched or read.

**Interviews**

Every conversation with SAS, a student, an instructor or anyone else gets a file in `research/interviews/` the same day, using the template there. Note which research questions A to K it answered, from `research/research-questions.md`.

# Two week plan, November 13 to 26

Goal. Find out whether the problem is real, define it with evidence, set requirements and scope, build and test prototypes, and collect evidence for the defense. Every team member has a task with a deadline every day. Every task is logged in `docs/daily-log.md` and committed by the person who did it.

Domain. Accessibility of learning material for blind and low vision university students.

Team. Abhinav Pandey, Shrijana Karki, Aakriti Mainali, Khemraj Adhikari. Abhinav does not work Saturday November 14 and works Sunday November 15.

## Method

Problem first, in this order. For whom, their objectives, their difficulties, why the difficulties exist, the consequences, and only then how to remove them. No solution is chosen before day 7.

| Phase | Days | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Problem research and validation | Nov 13 to 17 | Problem confirmed, changed or rejected with evidence |
| 2 Requirements and scope | Nov 18 to 19 | Problem statement, user needs, requirements, scope, success measures |
| 3 Design and prototypes | Nov 20 to 23 | Two or three prototypes, each tied to a requirement |
| 4 Evaluation and evidence | Nov 24 to 25 | Measured results against the success measures |
| 5 Defense preparation | Nov 26 | Final report, slides, demo |

## Rules
